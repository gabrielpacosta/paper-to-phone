Paper to Phone

Research papers reflowed for your phone, with every figure, table and reference. Your library lives in your own OneDrive.

  • Drop a PDF or a link from any device; it shows up here, ready to read.
  • Your place, theme and text size follow you between phone and computer.
  • Papers stay in your OneDrive. This app has no server and keeps nothing.

One-time setup. This copy of the app needs a Microsoft app ID before anyone can sign in. Paste it here once; the steps are in the README.

Works with personal Microsoft accounts and with work or school accounts that allow it.
